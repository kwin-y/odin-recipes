Lasagna

Home

Description

Classic lasagna is a layered baked pasta dish made with sheets of pasta, meat sauce, and layers of cheese. It's hearty, comforting, and great for feeding a crowd.

Ingredients

  • 1 box lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozarella
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredient, and preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Cook lasagna noodles according to package instruction.
  3. Brown the ground beef and mix with marinara sauce.
  4. Layer noodles, meat sauce, ricotta, and mozarella in a baking dish. Repeat layers.
  5. Top with parmesan and bake for 45 minutes.
  6. Allow lasagna to cool for at least 15 minutes before serving. Enjoy!
